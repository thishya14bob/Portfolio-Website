01 — Context

Why does language learning feel like homework?

Most language apps rely on gamified drills and flashcards — effective in the short term, but forgettable. Learners plateau, lose motivation, and abandon the habit before they can hold a real conversation.

We wanted to explore what language learning could feel like if it were as immersive as reading a novel you can't put down.

"The most effective way to learn a language is to be surrounded by it — through real context, not isolated vocabulary lists."

02 — The Solution

Language learning, one story at a time

Lingofable is a reading app where every story is written in your target language. Tap any word for an instant definition, track vocabulary automatically, and progress through levels as the stories get harder.

The design challenge was making this feel effortless — so learners stay in the reading flow without ever feeling like they're studying.
