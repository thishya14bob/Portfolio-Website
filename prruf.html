01. Where it started

We kept forgetting to log our work.

During an internship I had to keep a progress log. I tried dictating each task to Claude. It lasted two weeks, then I quietly stopped.

Later, Tanish mentioned he was facing the same issue. We spoke about it, and hearing it from him validated the idea for both of us.

"What if my laptop could just see what I'm doing and keep the notes itself?"
The abandoned two-week manual log → the Prruf dashboard

02. What it does

It takes your work notes for you.

Prruf is a small desktop app for Mac and Windows that sits quietly in the background. While it's on, every couple of minutes it quietly looks at your screen, works out what you're doing in words, and builds a running set of notes. No timers to start. No notes to write. No end-of-day catch-up.

When you need to show what you did to a client, a teammate, or just yourself next week, the notes are already there, ready to become a clean summary or a PDF.

Three screens: Sessions (dashboard) / Session (live capture) / Report (finished summary + Export PDF)

05. Not surveillance

Built for you, not your manager.

The Hubstaffs and Time Doctors of the world are employee-monitoring software. The employer installs them. Data flows up to a manager: stored screenshots, keystroke counts, productivity scores. The person on the keyboard is the subject. They're being watched by someone else.

Prruf is the opposite, on purpose. The safeguards came from real feedback, from people who said their fears out loud when we showed them early versions. Not from us anticipating them.

prruf: privacy rules

< privacy rules >

screenshot → memory only (never written to disk)
image → deleted immediately after read
stored → text only, always
data flows → nowhere. you own the record.
shared → only what you choose to share
Surveillance tools Prruf
Installed by the employer Installed by you, for you
Data flows up to a manager The record is yours. You edit it, you decide what's shared
Stores screenshots & keystrokes Only text is ever stored. Image deleted instantly.
Scores and ranks you No scores, no judgement. It just describes
You're the subject You're the owner
"The right mental model isn't a camera in the corner of the room. It's a personal observer sitting beside you, taking notes on your behalf. Notes that are yours to keep, correct, and share."
Observer, not warden: a small friendly character sitting next to a person at their desk, notepad in hand, facing the same direction

07. Why Prruf

Your work, your record.

We're not looking at what you do. Prruf is built for you, not for a manager or for us. It quietly keeps notes on your work, and what you do with them is entirely up to you.

Private by default: screenshots are never saved, and only text notes stay on your machine.

Nobody's watching: no manager dashboard, no scores, no one else looking in.

Yours to use: edit it, share it, send it to a client, or keep it for yourself.
